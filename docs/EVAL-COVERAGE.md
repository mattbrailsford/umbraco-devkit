## How to read this

- **Gain:** with-plugin score minus without-plugin score, from the latest recorded run
  (0–100 each). **Best gain** is the skill's highest, counting only evals where the skill
  loaded when it's proven. Guards for removed rules don't count.
- **Status, knowledge skills:** **Proven** = an eval gains 20 or more and the skill loaded
  in at least half its runs. **Gain, skill rarely loads** = the gain is there but the skill
  mostly didn't load, so it may come from elsewhere.
  **Small gain** = 10 to 19. **No gain** = under 10. **Unproven** = no eval
  with both scores.
- **Status, workflow skills:** they have no plain-Claude equivalent, so their evals check the
  skill keeps its own rules. Anything under 100 broke one.
- **Sections covered:** the skill's `## ` sections that at least one eval names in its
  `# covers:` line. A section with no eval is a rule nobody has proven is needed.
- **Context:** sections with an italic heading (`## *Background*`): background, scope,
  sources or a review list. They hold nothing an eval could fail, so they don't count.
- **Skill loaded:** in how many recorded with-plugin runs the skill actually loaded.
- **–** means nothing has been recorded.
